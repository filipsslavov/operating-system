Weekday schedule
Food — four rules

1 · Protein anchor. 40g+ per meal, 150–180g per day. Ordering out means the grilled thing, the bowl, the meat plus a side. This is what makes a deficit survivable.

2 · Three zero-effort defaults. One grocery run a week so "I don't want to decide" has an answer: Greek yogurt + frozen berries · rotisserie chicken + bagged salad · eggs + pre-cooked turkey sausage.

3 · No liquid calories Mon–Fri. Coffee black or a splash. Easy win, several hundred calories.

4 · Relocate the snacking. Nothing eaten standing in the kitchen — it goes on a plate and you sit. And anything you can't stop eating once opened doesn't live in the house. Inventory, not willpower.

Weekend drinking: two nights, not four. Mezcal + soda + lime is ~100 cal against ~280 for the margarita. Eat protein before, not after.

Target: bodyweight × 10 calories. Recalculate every 15 lbs. Track three weeks to calibrate your eye, then run on the rules.

Gym — A/B

3× a week, 45 minutes, 3 sets of 8–12. Hit 12 on all three sets, add weight next time. That progression is the entire program.

Workout A

  • Goblet squat
  • Dumbbell bench press
  • Seated cable row
  • Plank — 3 × 30–45 sec

Workout B

  • Romanian deadlift
  • Lat pulldown
  • Dumbbell shoulder press
  • Bulgarian split squat

Don't add exercises until month three. Variety is how gym programs die.

Project — 12 weeks, ships regardless

Wk 7–9
Analysis or model layer
Wk 10–11
Dashboard + written narrative
Wk 12
Publish. Whatever state it's in.

The four rules that make it stick:

  • One project at a time, with a ship date.
  • Every new idea goes to the parking lot. It does not get started. Look at the list on week 12 and not before.
  • Ship ugly. One finished thing beats four abandoned perfect ones — and it's the finished one you talk about in interviews.
  • Friday demo. Show someone what moved. External witness converts intention into completion.

The switching isn't a character flaw. It's what happens when a project has no deadline, no audience, and no cost to walking away.

Phone

  • Different room during deep blocks. Not face-down on the desk.
